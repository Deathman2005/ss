The Awadhi Legacy

A Pantry Revival Worth Savouring

"Because 70% of India eats non-veg, yet 90% of packaged food ignores them entirely."

Traditional Indian non-veg cooking is a slow, complex, and sacred craft. It requires roasting raw spices, grinding them by hand, and braising tender meats in heavy copper vessels over woodfires with dollops of real desi ghee.

In today's fast-paced world, this culture is disappearing. Convenience food has meant compromise—relying on chemical preservatives, high-sodium powders, and artificial additives.

Shahi Serve is fixing that, one heritage recipe at a time. We pack authentic, slow-cooked royal delicacies into clean-label, ready-to-eat jars. No preservatives. No fillers. Just taste that takes you home.

Our Heritage Process

The Journey of Royal Flavours

How we preserve 200-year-old Nawabi kitchen processes inside modern packaged convenience.

1
Step 1

Archival Recipe Sourcing

We dig deep into Awadhi, Deccani, and Mughlai culinary journals, interviewing generational royal Khansamas to capture true spice matrices.

2
Step 2

Braising in Pure Ghee

Our meats are hand-marinated for 12 hours, then slow-cooked in large brass cauldrons in small batches over several hours.

3
Step 3

Hermetic Retort Sealing

The cooked curry is packed into multi-layer pouches and pressure-sterilised, sealing in aromas and destroying micro-organisms.

4
Step 4

Pantry Ready in 2 Mins

Simply snip the pouch, heat it in a microwave or saucepan for 120 seconds, and experience history served hot.

We use a process called Retort Thermal Processing. Once cooked, the food is sealed inside multi-layer retort pouches to block air and light. The pouch is then steam-sterilised under high pressure, destroying all micro-organisms. It works just like home-canning, maintaining absolute freshness without a single preservative.
